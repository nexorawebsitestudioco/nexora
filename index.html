Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Nexora ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what information this website collects, why, and what you can do about it.

Information we collect

We only receive information you choose to send us through the contact form or by email:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your company name, project type and budget range (optional)
  • The project details you write

When you use the contact form, your details are sent to us through Formspree, a form service we use, and delivered to our email inbox. You can also email us directly.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss your project
  • To prepare a proposal or quote if you ask for one
  • To keep a record of our conversation with you

We do not sell your information, rent it, or use it for advertising.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels. If we add analytics later, we will update this page and say which tool we use.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts: the site loads its fonts from Google, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and your IP address is visible to them.
  • Formspree: processes contact form submissions for us and passes them to our email.
  • Email provider: when you email us, your message is handled by our email provider.

How long we keep your information

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to talk with you and handle any project we work on together. If we do not work together, we delete your enquiry within 12 months, or earlier if you ask.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we receive. No method of sending data over the internet is completely secure, so please do not send passwords or payment details by email.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. If you live in the EU, UK or California, you may have extra rights under local privacy laws, and we will honour them.

Children

This website is for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
